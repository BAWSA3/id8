# id8 · the 90-second walkthrough

For the demo recording and for anyone judging from the link. One continuous
take. Desktop, fresh browser (the desk tour plays on a first visit; skip it
if you want the short cut).

**Link:** https://id8-blush.vercel.app

## Beat 1, the door (5s)

Eclipse, wordmark, `a canvas for your thesis`. The boot readout ticks in
bottom-left: nansen smart money · live, 29 sectors, 26 chains, cohorts,
`writes your trade · never`. Click `[ open the desk ]`.

## Beat 2, the window (10s)

`the desk › what are we looking at?` Type a ticker, `NVDA` shows the new
Robinhood-chain coverage: `found · robinhood · mcap $13,727,783 · live`, then
`deepest pool NVDA/USDG · uniswap v3 · $7,803,515 liq · $60,510,242 24h`.
The eclipse lands on the seed. (Or `[ trading a narrative, not a name ]`.)

## Beat 3, present (10s)

`What's the play on $NVDA?` Type the thesis in your own words. The
authorship line reads `100% human, id8 never writes your thesis`.

Suggested thesis (live-tested):

> Tokenized equities on Robinhood chain are the next meta. NVDA is the
> deepest pool and the cleanest expression because it's the stock everyone
> already wants exposure to, and the USDG pair has real liquidity. I think
> onchain NVDA volume doubles in three weeks as the tokenized-stocks
> narrative rotates in from AI agents, and smart money is already positioning.

## Beat 4, clarify (25s)

The clarifier asks one question at a time, four at most, like a desk head:
what tells you it's forming rather than running; what takes you out; why
this vehicle; what has to happen in three weeks. Answer plainly. Then the
contract assembles: claim, vehicle, narrative, five assumptions each with
`from your words: "…"`, the invalidation, open questions. Strike a line if
it isn't what you meant. `[ take it to the board ]`.

## Beat 5, the board (20s)

The tape lands live: the planner picked the `Tokenized Stocks` sector, the
analyst reports `NVDA 7d netflow -$3,759,007 · smart traders -$1,354 across
96 wallets · fresh wallets +$47,556,789`, and the skeptic asks what flow
print inside three weeks says the rotation isn't happening. Click an EV
node: the dossier shows exactly which assumption it tests and the verdict.
`[ make the ruling ]`.

## Beat 6, the ruling (15s)

Rule on the contested lines. Hold one against the tape and write why. Cut
one. The board settles beside you as you rule. The invalidation is already
there in your words. `[ put it on the book ]`.

## Beat 7, on the book (5s)

The doc: thesis after the tape, the ledger, every line with its receipts.
`[ copy a link ]`, the link is the doc. Paste it anywhere; it unfurls.

## The one-liner

Receipts for judgment. FOMO gives traders receipts for trades; id8 gives
them receipts for the thesis *before* the trade, interrogated, tested
against Nansen cohort flows, ruled on in their own words, and shareable.
It never writes the trade.
